Smartwatches aren’t so bad

I was gifted a smartwatch not too long ago; a xiaomi smartband 9 pro. And unlike the name suggests, it’s not a band, it’s not proffesional gear, but it is from the xiaomi brand, I’ll give em that. I was hesitent how much I’d use it with what I knew about the tool, but will you lookit I’m like the dad that never wanted the family dog prior to acquiring it.

Smartwatches are just minature bellhops strapped to your wrist bones re-sounding every notification you got on your phone, no? No, they are not (usually).

They’re actually pretty rad

The sports focused ones turn out to be more or less pip boys, like mine can keep a to-do-list, map my walk routes, and give me a bright green percentage of how likely I am to hit my laundry basket –as well as remind me to do the laundry at a given time and weekday–. It also lasts heaps for what it is, it’s going at a pace of about -20% charge per 5 days. That’s 25 days a charge for the folk who don’t have calculators on their wrists, that includes me. Who has a compass but not a calculator app? Xiaomi by the looks of it.

There’s one con though… It don’t have no games on it :(
